# IT Labor Market Analysis

## Objective

Collect and analyze 10 current IT vacancies related to:

- Project Manager
- Delivery Manager
- Product Owner
- Business Analyst / PM

## Sources

1. hh.kz
2. LinkedIn
3. Company career pages
4. Relevant Telegram vacancy channels

## Selection criteria

Each vacancy must contain:
- company
- position
- source
- responsibilities
- requirements
- tools
- experience requirements
- language requirements
